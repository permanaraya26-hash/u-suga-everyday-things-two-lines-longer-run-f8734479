A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479) bounty entry: a program that writes a two-line explanation of one everyday thing to a 10-year-old to `/output/entry.txt` — line 1 a vivid analogy, line 2 an honest sentence about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. No dependencies, no network, deterministic — every run produces the same bytes.

## The entry

> A toaster is a little elevator for bread: it carries your slice down into a hot, glowing box and pops it back up brown and crunchy.
> The ride is the true part — the bread goes down, waits, and pops back up — but the elevator leaves out the heat that browns it and the fact that bread never chooses the trip.

Two lines, 306 characters. Line 1 explains a toaster through an elevator a 10-year-old can stand in; line 2 names what the ride maps and the two things it leaves out: the heat, and the bread's lack of choice.
